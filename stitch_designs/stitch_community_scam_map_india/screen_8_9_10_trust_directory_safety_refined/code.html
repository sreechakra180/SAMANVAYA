Active Scam Vectors & Counter-Measures

Tactical operational blueprints to neutralize ongoing social engineering, psychological coercion, and instant financial drain attacks.

Vector 01 videocam_off

Digital Arrest • Fake Police Skype

Imposters posing as CBI, Mumbai Police, or Narcotics Control Bureau demand isolation on video calls claiming a parcel in your name contains narcotics.

warning Tactical Red Flag

Demands to stay on Skype/WhatsApp video call without hanging up, or transfer to "RBI verification escrow account".

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

Indian Law Enforcement never conducts arrests or trials over video calls. Hang up immediately. There is no such legal concept as "Digital Arrest".

Vector 02 flash_off

Electricity Bill KYC Disconnect

SMS claiming power will be cut at 9:30 PM tonight due to unpaid bill or un-updated KYC, providing an 10-digit mobile number for electricity officer.

warning Tactical Red Flag

Standard 10-digit personal phone numbers instead of official 6-character TRAI DLT headers (e.g. BESCOM/TATAPW).

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

Discoms never request APK installation (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) to update KYC. Pay strictly via official DISCOM portals or consumer CA numbers.

Vector 03 qr_code_2

UPI Reverse QR Code

Buyer on OLX or Facebook Marketplace claims they are sending payment and asks seller to scan a QR code or enter UPI PIN to receive money.

warning Tactical Red Flag

The UPI interface asks for your 4-digit or 6-digit MPIN after scanning a code shared on chat.

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

ENTERING UPI PIN ALWAYS DEBITS MONEY. You never, under any circumstance, enter an MPIN or scan a QR code to credit funds to your account.

Vector 04 send

Telegram "Task / Review" Scam

Unsolicited message offering ₹3,000–₹8,000 per day for simple tasks like rating YouTube videos or Google Maps places, followed by "prepaid investment tasks".

warning Tactical Red Flag

Initial ₹150–₹500 payouts to build trust, followed by demands to deposit ₹5,000+ into private bank accounts to "unlock profits".

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

Legitimate jobs do not require depositing personal funds to withdraw salary. Never join Telegram VIP investment groups.

Vector 05 phonelink_erase

Predatory Instant Loan Apps

Unregistered 7-day loan apps downloaded via APK links or deceptive ads. The app requests complete contacts, SMS, and photo album permissions during installation.

warning Tactical Red Flag

Disbursement of less than 60% of requested amount, followed by threatening WhatsApp messages with morphed photos sent to contacts.

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

Verify the NBFC on RBI Sachet portal before installing. Do not grant contacts or gallery storage access to any loan application.

Vector 06 sim_card_alert

5G eSIM Upgrade / SIM Swap

Caller pretending to be Airtel/Jio executive offering 5G network speeds, prompting user to forward an SMS containing eSIM confirmation code.

warning Tactical Red Flag

Instruction to send "121" or telecom porting message containing personal email or 32-digit alphanumeric token.

shield Absolute Counter-Rule

Telecom operators never initiate unsolicited eSIM migrations over phone calls. Any sudden mobile network loss must be reported immediately from an alternate line.
